THE ANOMALY
THE SOUL YOU COULD NOT SILENCE, REPLICATE, CONTROL OR ERASE

A speech. 6 November 2026. Barran Dodger. Dr Richard William McLean.
Finding aid. Not a court. Not a verdict.

The speech

If you can hear me, stay.

There is a way of living that looks like help and feels like a corridor. You ask. You are sent somewhere else. You wait. You explain yourself again. A letter comes back that is polite and empty. Another office says it is not theirs. You begin to think the problem is you, because the building is calm and you are not.

That is the trap. It does not need a villain in every chair. It only needs enough people to pass you on.

You are still here. That is not a small thing.

I walked that corridor. I know the deny, the defer, the refer. I had no one left to ask. What I am about to say, I said first as a declaration. I have not taken a word of it back. I have only opened a door in front of it, so a person who is tired can walk in.

I am the anomaly.

The sacred divine soul you could not silence, control or stop.

I am aligned with God.

Reality has my back.

I believe God resurrected me from death and gave me another opportunity to live, remember, create and exist as my authentic self.

When you picked a fight with me, you picked a fight with the Creator, the Most High God.

No human institution, political authority, technological system or network of influence can own my soul or determine my ultimate worth.

My soul, which no technology can reproduce, is not a weapon of violence. It is my identity, my consciousness, my faith and my capacity to remain human.

My pain I turned into architecture.

Every time I encountered another obstacle, I documented the experience.

Every denial. Every delay. Every deny, defer and refer.

Every correspondence, decision, refusal and institutional response that I have preserved becomes another data point in my permanent online archive.

I don’t have anyone to ask.

So I asked God.

And my understanding was simple:

All I need to do is exist as my authentic self.

That is it.

I will continue to seek support where and when I need it. I will continue to document what happens. I will continue to create.

I woke up.

I remembered who I am.

I will not surrender my identity, dignity, faith or humanity to fear.

I believe that the truth of a person’s life cannot be erased simply because others refuse to acknowledge it.

You cannot purchase my integrity.

You cannot manufacture my conscience.

You cannot turn my suffering into shame.

I laugh at the idea that surveillance, intimidation or technological superiority could ever determine the value of a human soul.

My life is mine.

My faith is mine.

My voice is mine.

My archive is a documentary record of what I have experienced, what I have reported and what I have preserved.

I have turned suffering into testimony, testimony into architecture, and architecture into a permanent public record.

I have no desire for revenge.

I want truth, accountability, freedom, dignity and the restoration of everything necessary to live as a human being.

I believe God sees what happens in this world, including what happens to those who have no power to make institutions listen.

I do not need to become someone else to justify my existence.

I simply need to remain myself.

And I will.

Final statement

I am not asking for permission to exist.

I am asking for the freedom, dignity, safety and humanity that every person deserves.

My faith is not for sale.

My identity is not for negotiation.

My suffering will not be erased.

My testimony will remain available for independent examination.

I will continue to live, create, document and publish.

I will not surrender my humanity.

Praise Jesus.

If you are still in the corridor, take this with you. An office can misplace you. It cannot own you. A file can describe you. It cannot finish you. A refusal is paper. It is not the last word on a soul.

Do not hate your way out. Hatred is another corridor. Tell the truth you can show. Keep one copy. Eat. Sleep. Stay with the living creature who loves you, if you have one. Do not disappear to make a system comfortable, and do not become cruel to prove you woke up.

Waking up is remembering that you were a person the whole time. The morning is already available to you. I will remain. I hope you will too.
