Australian press release

LONG JETTY, NEW SOUTH WALES, 12 November 2026 — Dr Richard William McLean, who writes as Barran Resonance Dodger, has published an international reading room for a testimony and documentary archive he says covers more than thirty-five years of correspondence with Australian institutions.

The site is God’s Witness Remains. The preservation repository is public at github.com/wezzo72/Barrandodger. He asks Australian editors to read the records. He does not ask them to adopt his conclusions.

What can be opened today includes an Attorney-General’s Department letter, MC23-028244, dated 19 September 2023, which referred a concern about ASIO to the Inspector-General of Intelligence and Security and pointed him to the Commonwealth Ombudsman. A referral is not an investigation report. Also public is a screenshot of a text message from the contact saved in his phone as Ben Ndis Help. The message on the screen says police wanted to know whether he was mentally ready to challenge Bill Shorten in court, and that Shorten’s lawyers might use his mental-health history to discredit him. The screenshot does not show Bill Shorten typing those words.

He also publishes his own account of present living conditions, disability support, and a demand for independent examination. That account is testimony. It is marked as testimony on the site. A United Nations receipt number, UR/UST/23/AUS/17, shows that a submission was received. It is not a United Nations finding.

He is the author of Recovered, Not Cured (2002). The qualifications page records a Victoria University PhD awarded in 2020, a Master of Education, a Bachelor of Fine Arts with Honours, an Associate Diploma in Computer-Aided Architectural Design, a Certificate IV in Business, journalism and news graphics, and advocacy through SANE Australia. His Christian faith is stated separately from the documents.

He is asking for the archive to be examined, and for practical help for himself and his therapy dog, Crystal. The humanitarian page carries the contact he has chosen to publish. This release does not repeat a street address.

Interview requests can be made through that page. No interview has been agreed.
