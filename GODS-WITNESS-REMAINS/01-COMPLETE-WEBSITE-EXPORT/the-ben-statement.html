Ben wrote it. I am the truth of it.

Dr Richard William McLean. Barran Dodger. I do not need a lawyer, a cop, a tribunal or a minister to decide whether these messages exist. They exist. I received them. I am publishing them.

Ben was my NDIS provider. He was paid to assist me. Months apart, he wrote two chains. The first put the police, a court, Bill Shorten and my mental-health history in one message. The later chain is the close call, the sex the police called consensual, the woman he said was paid to discredit me, the corruption he said went to the top, the hit he was afraid of, and the agreement he said would get him charged if he spoke.

The first chain. Bill Shorten.

I wrote:

Ben wrote:

I wrote:

Yes

But I know they were going that already.

That's why I e absconded.

The last line is cut off on the screen. I will not invent the rest of it. What is there is enough. I absconded because they were going to stitch me up as the mad one, and I wrote that they were already doing it.

Bill Shorten was the NDIS minister. That office had a duty to a participant. Ben was not the minister and he was not the police. He was the provider, and he carried their question to me: was I mentally ready to challenge Shorten, because his lawyers might use my history to discredit the story. That is the weapon. The diagnosis instead of the documents. I hold Shorten responsible for the AVO and the exile that used that same power. This screen is the method, in writing, from the man paid to be on my side.

Months later. The close call.

The report they paid for. The police called the sex consensual.

All the way to the top. The hit. The agreement. Treason.
