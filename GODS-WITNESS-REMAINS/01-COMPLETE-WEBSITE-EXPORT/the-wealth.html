Four kinds of value, kept apart

  • Historical financial value. Losses and economic consequences that can be tied to a record.
  • Intellectual and cultural value. The archive, the original writings, the gospels, the prophetic literature, the artwork, and the preservation.
  • Commercial value. What original work could earn by publishing, licensing, sales, membership and sponsorship, without locking the evidence.
  • Humanitarian and legal value. Remedies that may exist if a particular allegation is established. An allegation is not an entitlement.

An archive can be culturally and commercially valuable without a court having established its allegations. The author’s description of himself as God’s chosen witness is his spiritual testimony. It is examined here as literature, theology and culture. It is not certified as divine appointment.

The programme — reports 01 to 15

These are the reports still to be built. They are not finished audits. Each one needs its own sources, method, schedule and limit.

01 — Master forensic financial assessment

A reconstruction of direct money lost, lost earnings, housing and displacement, legal and medical cost, lost future capacity, and inflation. Every figure has to name its source, its sum, its assumption, and whether it has already been counted.

02 — Economic consequences of alleged institutional detriment

The alleged cumulative cost of exclusion, poverty, lost services and blocked remedies. Individual decisions, institutional conduct, and any claim of coordination stay separate.

03 — Whole-of-system public cost

A model of what repeated admissions, reviews, complaints, housing and disability processes may have cost the public. A cost model is not a finding that an agency intended the cost.

04 — Historical financial reconstruction, 1990–2026

A dated ledger. Each event that can be dated is tied to the original record and to any measurable money consequence. What cannot be tied is marked absent.

05 — Reconciliation of the earlier figures

The project has referred to an AU$32.9 million calculation in Report 08, and a police-report PDF has referred to an AU$43 million to AU$139 million compensation range. Neither figure is adopted here. Neither is an established entitlement. The work of Report 05 is to say, line by line, what can be reproduced, what is on an invoice, what is calculated, what is estimated, what is alleged, what is duplicated, and what is unsubstantiated.

06 — Documentary archive valuation

Value the collection as a compiled record: size, originality of the compilation, primary sources, indexing, preservation and access. Separate the collection, the original writing, the machinery of the site, and the preservation work. Low, central and high. No single invented headline.

07 — Literary intellectual property

An inventory of original books, gospels, prophetic papers, essays, testimony, speeches and digital publications, with possible publishing, translation and adaptation income. Copyright in an original sentence is not copyright in a government letter collected beside it.

08 — Sacred literature

A separate catalogue for the spiritual works, including the gospels, Atherion Witnessed, the Gospel of the Eliven Chain, the Manifesto of the Unerased, the Church of Barran Dodger, God’s Chosen Witness, and the Justice Mandate, where those works can be opened. Sacred meaning is not a market price.

09 — Visual art

Original drawings, covers, emblems, video and site identity, each with title, date, medium and file. Government documents are not sold as original art.

10 — Rights register

Who owns what, what can be licensed, and what must not be assigned away to get a book printed. Australian copyright in an original literary or artistic work can arise without registration. That is a starting point, not a valuation. See the Copyright Act 1968.

11 — Compensation and damages

Every potential claim needs a respondent, a conduct, a record, a causation, a loss and a remedy. A description of conduct as torture, corruption or an assassination attempt does not by itself create a legal entitlement. The categories to investigate, and not to assume, include negligence, clinical injury, acquired brain injury, psychiatric injury, assault, false imprisonment, defamation, disability discrimination, whistleblower detriment, maladministration, privacy, financial misconduct, housing harm, and failure of a contracted service.

12 — Human rights and humanitarian impact

The documented circumstances, the alleged violations, and the help required now. The humanitarian SOS is on its own page. It is not a footnote to a valuation.

13 — Publishing house

The publishing house already has a page: Publishing HouseGlobal Publishing House. The commercial rule is fixed. The evidence remains free. Original creative work can be sold. Do not sign away all rights to be published. Do not sell another person’s letter, or a government file, as if it were an original book.

14 — Income that can be repeated

Book sales, prints of original art, voluntary membership that does not hide the evidence, ethical sponsorship with no editorial control, donations, grants, licences, talks and courses. Donations, sales and restricted funds are different ledgers. An annual statement should show which is which.

15 — Church, ministry and trust

The Church of Barran Dodger, the free archive, the publishing trade, and any humanitarian fund should not be one pocket. Charity registration in Australia is a separate legal question and is not automatic. A charity is not a personal profit vehicle. This page does not register one.

The hospital record and the brain injury

His testimony on this site says he was held under the Mental Health Act sixteen times in about five years, and that a suicide attempt left a cognitive brain injury for which no compensation was paid. An earlier archive paper has been said to count fourteen admissions. Fourteen and sixteen are not the same number. The difference is to be reconciled from admission and discharge records. It is not to be smoothed.

A medical injury, its cause, and its financial consequence are three questions. Pain, loss of enjoyment, past and future earnings, care, rehabilitation, accommodation and travel are separate lines. The same injury is not counted four times under four names.

If you are in immediate danger, call 000. Lifeline is 13 11 14. This page mentions a suicide attempt because he required that fact to remain. It does not describe a method.
