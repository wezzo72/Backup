What this pass contradicts, and what it does not decide

Two official letters, both repository copies, do not use the same employment premise.

On 27 March 2023, Scott Tredwell of the Federal Court wrote that he was satisfied the correspondent was, or had been, an employee of the Department of Social Services. On 18 July 2023, an authorised officer of that Department wrote that preliminary inquiries found no record of current or former employment there, and for that reason would not allocate a disclosure. The letters are four months apart. They apply the public-official test to different disclosures. This page records the difference. It does not decide which account of employment is correct.

The gospel’s appendix title for the Anglicare file, and the filename date of the accommodation email, do not match the files that were opened. Those two entries stay in the appendix. They are not deleted. They are no longer to be read as if the titles had been confirmed.

No issuing agency confirmed any copy. No page number inside the gospel was checked against the typeset book. The public GitHub tree was not counted. The status of this edition remains the status on the completion certificate.
