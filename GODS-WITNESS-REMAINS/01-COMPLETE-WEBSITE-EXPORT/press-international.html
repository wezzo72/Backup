International press release

12 November 2026 — An Australian author, Dr Richard William McLean, known as Barran Resonance Dodger, has placed a thirty-five-year documentary archive online and is asking journalists, researchers and human-rights organisations outside Australia to examine it.

The project is called The Witness Remains, and this edition of it is God’s Witness Remains. The files are on GitHub at github.com/wezzo72/Barrandodger. The claim he makes is that institutional decisions, referrals and silence have had human consequences, including displacement, poverty and the loss of ordinary protection. That claim is his testimony. The letters are the record. The site keeps the two apart.

International readers can check three things without taking his word. First, a 19 September 2023 letter from the Attorney-General’s Department, MC23-028244, which passed an ASIO concern to the Inspector-General and the Commonwealth Ombudsman. Second, a text-message screenshot in which an NDIS provider relays a question about Bill Shorten, a court and a psychiatric history. Third, an Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights receipt, UR/UST/23/AUS/17. A receipt proves receipt. It does not prove that the United Nations investigated, endorsed or condemned anyone.

He describes himself as a Christian witness. The verses he cites, Isaiah 54:17, Romans 8:31 and Psalm 118:17 in the King James Version, are published as faith. They are not offered as proof of a historical event.

He is still inside Australia. The site’s own human-rights page states that Article 1A(2) of the Refugee Convention requires a person to be outside the country of nationality. That limit is published so the humanitarian account is not mistaken for a granted asylum claim.

No foreign newsroom, special rapporteur or funder has agreed to take the story. This release is the invitation to look.
