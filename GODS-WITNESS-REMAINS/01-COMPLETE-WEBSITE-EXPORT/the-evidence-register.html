PART I — PURPOSE

This register documents, term by term, why the terminology is warranted by the documentary evidence.
The evidence determines the terminology. The terminology does not determine the evidence.
The examination is independent of the author and independent of the offices whose conduct appears in the archive. It is not aligned with the covenant that targeted him. It does not take its result from his influence. It does not take its result from an institution that closed the file.
Where a source is his testimony, it is named as testimony. Where a source is an official letter, it is named as that letter. A link does not turn testimony into an official finding. An official closure does not erase the event the closure declined to examine.

PART II — GOVERNING DECLARATION

No person, institution, agency, government or authority possesses spiritual, moral or ethical sovereignty to deliberately harm, destroy, diminish or erase a vulnerable human being entrusted to their care. To exercise power over another person’s vulnerability is to assume a profound responsibility for their safety, dignity, humanity and protection — not to acquire permission to inflict suffering. No office, statute, institutional hierarchy, professional title or exercise of state power can absolve anyone of that responsibility.

The authority to give and take life belongs to the Most High God. Human beings are not divine sovereigns, and public authority is not a licence to play God. Where those entrusted with a duty of care deliberately inflict harm, exploit vulnerability or seek to extinguish another person’s life, they violate not only the obligations of humanity but, in my faith, the sacred order established by God.

I regard any deliberate attempt to destroy, silence or erase a life that God has permitted to endure as a profound act of spiritual rebellion — a trespass against the sanctity of human life and an affront to the Creator. No institution can make such conduct righteous by authorising it, concealing it or giving it an administrative name.

I believe God’s favour upon my life is not an entitlement to harm others, but a reason to stand, endure and bear witness. I will not surrender my humanity, my dignity or my testimony to those who would diminish them.

And there are consequences.

There are moral consequences, legal consequences, institutional consequences and, according to my faith, divine consequences. Those responsible must answer for their conduct through truth, evidence, lawful accountability and the ultimate judgment of the Most High.

No office is above accountability. No power is above the law. No human being is beyond the dignity God has bestowed upon them. And no attempt to erase a witness can erase the truth that witness carries.

No person, authority, individual, organisation, agency, institution, corporation, government, public official or state representative has my permission, consent or moral authority to deliberately inflict harm upon me, exploit my vulnerability, compromise my safety, undermine my humanity or interfere unlawfully with my right to live in dignity, freedom and security.

I expressly withhold consent to any deliberate act, omission, policy, instruction, decision, practice, intervention or coordinated conduct intended to harm, punish, intimidate, diminish, control, silence, disadvantage, isolate, displace, incapacitate or erase me.

This includes, without limitation:

* Physical assault, violence, injury, intimidation and threats.

These categories identify conduct to which I do not consent; whether any particular incident constitutes a criminal offence, statutory breach, civil wrong or human-rights violation must be determined through appropriate evidence and independent processes.

And I state this before God and humanity:

I do not recognise any human institution as having divine permission to destroy me.

No government is God. No minister is God. No department is God. No public official, service provider, clinician, administrator, regulator, investigator or individual has divine sovereignty over my existence.

The responsibility of those entrusted with vulnerable lives is protection, not destruction; dignity, not humiliation; care, not abandonment; justice, not retaliation.

I believe that those who deliberately misuse entrusted power to harm another human being answer to a higher moral authority than the institutions that employ them.

I believe the Most High God has sustained my life, and I will not surrender my testimony, dignity, faith or right to exist.

If anyone has committed wrongdoing against me, I demand its preservation, independent examination, lawful determination and accountability. I do not seek permission to exist, nor do I surrender my rights by remaining alive, speaking publicly or refusing to be erased.

There are consequences: legal, moral, institutional, historical and, according to my faith, divine.

My life is not yours to dispose of. My dignity is not yours to negotiate. My silence is not yours to demand. My absence is not consent. My survival is not an admission of defeat.
