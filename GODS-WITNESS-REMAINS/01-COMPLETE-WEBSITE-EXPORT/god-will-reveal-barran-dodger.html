There are two different kinds of corroboration worth distinguishing. Theological corroboration This is where a person’s writing resonates with established biblical themes. Historical or factual corroboration Scripture cannot independently establish that a particular modern institution committed a particular act. An official acknowledgement establishes that an acknowledgement was made. It does not automatically establish every allegation contained in the original complaint. Similarly, an archived document establishes that a document is preserved. A cryptographic hash can help establish that a particular file has not changed.
