PREAMBLE — THE QUESTION OF HUMAN VALUE

A person’s worth is not determined by the institution that administers their money, the provider that supplies their accommodation, the clinician who records their diagnosis, the public servant who makes a decision about them, or the government that funds their support. Nor is worth established by qualifications, titles, publications or recognition alone. Human value is inherent. Achievements demonstrate contribution. Qualifications demonstrate education. Experience demonstrates activity. Conduct demonstrates character. Decisions demonstrate responsibility. Consequences demonstrate why accountability matters. The same principles apply to everyone examined here.

Richard William McLean is not entitled to have every allegation accepted merely because he is the person making it. An institution is not entitled to have its decisions accepted merely because it possesses official authority. The evidence, not the status of the person presenting it, determines what can be established. The conclusions below are direct. They distinguish established documentary facts, recorded testimony, corroborated material, and matters that remain unproven. His testimony is not erased by the last of those categories. An official reply is not an adequate reply. A title is not moral superiority.

What was opened: the Victoria University repository, the 2003 book’s bibliographic record and the Disability Studies Quarterly review, McLean and Comcare [2023] AATA 2096, the Parliamentary Handbook entry for Bill Shorten, the NDIS Commission page for Ablepoint Australia, the NDIS Code of Conduct, section 39 of the NSW Trustee and Guardian Act 2009, and the published biography of Sukhi Tear. The archive at https://github.com/wezzo72/Barrandodger was inspected as a public body of records, not read PDF by PDF. The vehicle decision, the section 122 PDF and the coordinator’s case notes were not extracted line by line. That limit is stated once. It is not a favour to the offices.

DOCUMENT 2 — CHARACTER AND GOVERNING PRINCIPLES

The archive invokes truth, humanity, integrity, independence, justice, dignity, courage, transparency and accountability. The question is whether those values appear in the work.

Integrity and transparency are demonstrated by a public archive that includes the qualifications, the allegations, the disputed issues and the limits. Honesty as an inner quality cannot be measured from publications alone. Each factual sentence remains testable, and he has put the sentences where they can be tested. Accountability is demonstrated by correspondence that names people, institutions, decisions and dates and asks for an explanation. Authenticity is the consistent use of his own experience. Compassion and empathy are in the mental-health writing and the therapeutic work. They are not a certificate for every relationship. Humanity, dignity, equality and respect for life are the demand to be treated as a person rather than a file. Justice and fairness are the demand for review under one standard.

Courage is the public discussion of schizophrenia, disability and institutional conflict. Intellectual curiosity is the two theses. Independence and creativity are the art, the books and the archive. Passion, persistence and resilience are the chronology itself, continued through the adversity he reports. Loyalty, on his own terms, is to his ethical and spiritual commitments. Truthfulness is expressed and remains subject to evidence. Openness is the publication. Relevance is the subject matter: disability, human worth, and technology. Responsibility is the labour of preserving his own account. Service is the advocacy and the provider work the Tribunal recorded. Public interest is the direction of the archive toward the conduct of public administration.

His tone can be forceful. That is visible. It does not make the complaints false, and it does not excuse an inaccurate claim if one is made. Spiritual and prophetic writings are his religious understanding. They are not treated as independent evidence of divine appointment or of a supernatural event. The strongest evidence of character is not the intensity of a declaration. It is the research completed, the book released, and the records preserved.

DOCUMENT 4 — CIRCUMSTANCES AND THE HUMAN COST

He has reported schizophrenia, ADHD, anxiety, depression, PTSD and an acquired brain injury. These are health matters. They explain support needs. They do not decide the truth of a financial decision, a service dispute or a complaint. A person with schizophrenia can accurately describe a ledger. The response required is to examine the evidence and to support communication. It is not to use the diagnosis as the explanation of every disagreement. He is to be assessed as a whole person, with support needs and decision-making rights both recognised.

The recorded plan for 20 November 2025 to 19 November 2026, as set out in the case history on this record, totals $366,406.69. Core flexible $274,255.84. Behaviour support $27,958.80. Improved daily living $11,140.53. Improved living arrangements $4,163.12. Support coordination and psychosocial recovery coaches $47,635.00. Choice and control $1,253.40. Those lines add to the total. They should be reconciled against the original approval, which was not re-extracted tonight. He reports that the practical support and the housing did not meet what he needed, and that a section 48 outcome did not provide the SDA, the 24-hour SIL or the respite he sought. A large allocation does not prove adequate support. A disagreement does not, by itself, prove the Agency acted unlawfully. The question that warrants scrutiny is whether the money became safe, appropriate, participant-directed support.

On his account, it did not.

He reports income of about $1,371.27 a fortnight and recorded expenses leaving about $1.31, a reduced allowance, difficulty with online banking, and the loss of the vehicle. These are his reported figures, not an audited trustee statement. Their significance is plain. If he cannot reach money, transport or another bed, a formal freedom to change providers has little real meaning. Financial administration that ignores that consequence undermines the self-reliance section 39 requires. The full ledger, and intentional deprivation by a named officer, are not established by the pages opened. The autonomy and welfare concern is substantial and is not minimised.

Crystal is his eight-year-old blue-eyed Siberian husky. He has identified her welfare, and the life they had including a period in a vehicle that he experienced as a kind of freedom later lost, as central. That testimony is not dismissed because an administrator would have chosen differently. The question is whether his wishes, her welfare, and a safe alternative were actually considered. They are a material part of any accommodation decision.

The 18 November 2026 complaint is a safeguarding record. It identifies rosters, attendance, case notes, claims, incident records and internal communications as the documents that can resolve the dispute. It proves the concerns were raised. It is not, alone, proof of every event. The specificity matters. The matters are not beyond verification. Acknowledgement is not resolution.

DOCUMENT 5 — THE ETHICAL CONTRADICTION REGISTER

Respect for self-determination, against his report of little practical control over money, housing and support. Least restrictive administration, against restricted access and reduced independence. Safe and competent support, against disputed delivery. Integrity and transparency, against difficulty identifying who decided. Prompt action on safety, against repeated complaints without an integrated answer. Choice and control, against coordination he disputes. Dignity, against reduction to a classification. Complaints handling, against acknowledgements and referrals that do not resolve the problem. Financial welfare, against the constraint he reports. Professional advocacy, against coordination he says did not serve him.

Section 39 and the Code are not ambiguous. They do not, by themselves, prove every alleged breach. The strongest contradiction on the available record is the gap between the language of independence and his reported experience of diminished practical independence. That gap is serious. It deserves a direct answer. It is not answered by a registration, a funding total, a case number, or a completed process.

DOCUMENT 6 — THE HUMANITY TEST

Education: two university research theses, including a doctorate, against qualifications that vary and are verified only where a public record was opened. Shorten’s degrees are on the Parliamentary Handbook. Tear’s diploma, degree and deferred master’s are on the published biography. Others were not supplied with degrees they have not published. Professional experience: art, writing, disability support, advocacy and research, against statutory, clinical and service roles. Intellectual contribution: the research, the books, the art and the archive, against public administration. Ethical responsibility differs: he is responsible for accuracy in what he publishes; they are responsible for decisions, services, records and safeguarding. Transparency: he published the archive; they owe explanations and records.

Power: he bears the practical effects; they exercise authority that can change a life. Contribution: research, art, literature and preservation, against services that are a contribution only when they protect the person.

This does not give him greater legal authority than a minister or a trustee. It means human worth is equal and responsibility is not. The greater the power, the greater the duty. A qualification is not moral superiority. Office is not immunity. Disability is not lesser worth.

The human question is whether a man who had published, researched, worked in the sector and made art was treated as that person, or only as a category. The available material supports that he has repeatedly asked for the whole circumstance to be seen. It also records process, jurisdiction and referral. A fragmented system can fail a person without every officer conspiring. A process can be narrowly correct and still harmful in total. An institution can answer a letter and not answer the problem. The concern supported here is fragmentation, burden, and the absence of one demonstrated resolution of money, housing, support and safety together. That is not dressed up as a secret plot. It is also not dressed down into a minor inconvenience.

DOCUMENT 7 — THE FINAL FINDING

Established. The 2020 PhD and the 2015 research master’s thesis. The 2019 NDIS provider registration and the therapeutic-support work, as recorded by the Tribunal. The 2003 book. A long artistic and public chronology in his historical profile, not every line independently audited. A large public archive, not every file authenticated. Multiple institutions with real power over his circumstances. Explicit duties under section 39 and the Code. Formal complaints about money, accommodation, the provider, coordination and the responses. Ablepoint’s registration and conditions. Tear’s published qualifications and commitments. Shorten’s ministerial tenure and his university degrees, as the Handbook records them.

Serious, and not adjudicated as a completed legal breach. The reported restriction of money and its effect on ordinary living. The reported loss of transport. The reported difficulty of leaving a placement he says he does not want. The scale of the plan against the support he says he did not receive. The disputed shift and its recording. Coordination he says ignored his preferences. The cumulative referrals. The absence of one integrated resolution. The distance between a statute of self-reliance and a life he describes as constrained. The need to name the individuals who decided. These are not trivial. They are the conditions of a dignified life.

Preserved, and not established as fact by the material opened. Allegations of political targeting, coordinated institutional conduct, deliberate persecution, covert surveillance, V2K, assassination-related conduct, corruption and other organised wrongdoing. They remain his testimony. The material examined does not independently establish them as facts. Complaint correspondence and process records do not, by themselves, establish bribery, covert operations, media suppression, a coordinated conspiracy, or the use of V2K. That distinction does not invalidate his account of his own experiences. It does not excuse failures that ordinary administrative, financial, disability-support or safeguarding records can establish. It stops an unsupported allegation being presented as a proved finding. No institution receives immunity. No allegation receives automatic confirmation.

Comparative conclusion. He has made a real contribution to intellectual, artistic, literary and disability-related life in Australia. He is not the diagnosis in the file, not the funding total, not the resident of a placement. The achievements do not make him superior to other people. They do make the reduction of his identity to a label indefensible. The same measure binds the others. A minister’s title, a trustee’s statute, a coordinator’s qualification, a provider’s registration and a clinician’s status do not prove ethical excellence. Worth is equal. Duty is not. The measure of their standing is how they use the power.

Accountability finding. There is a substantial gap between the ideals of autonomy, dignity, independence and safeguarding and the practical life he describes. He has repeatedly raised it. Several systems have been involved. Their own instruments contain the obligations. The material does not establish that every named person acted unlawfully or deliberately caused the circumstances. It also does not establish that they collectively resolved the substantive problems. That absence of a demonstrated, comprehensive resolution is the central accountability failure visible here. A person should not have to master every system to obtain the ordinary conditions of dignity. He should not have to re-explain his identity to offices that hold his records. He should not have to prove his humanity with a doctorate before a complaint is taken seriously.

He should not have to surrender independence in order to receive assistance. A right that exists on paper and cannot be used is not a right in practice. These are basic requirements of humane administration.

Final verdict. THE HUMAN BEING MUST COME BEFORE THE ADMINISTRATION. The evidence establishes the achievements, the authority exercised over him, the duties, and serious concerns about money, housing, support and complaint handling. It does not establish every allegation of deliberate coordination or criminal conduct. It does establish that the concerns cannot responsibly be dismissed as mere dissatisfaction or reduced to his disability.

The value of his life is not the money administered for him, not the room assigned to him, not the convenience of his correspondence, and not whether a government finds his testimony comfortable. Humanity is not a credential. Integrity is not a job title. Accountability is not a courtesy. The greatest responsibility belongs to those entrusted with power over another person’s life. They must use it to protect dignity, not diminish it; to support independence, not restrict it without necessity; to answer a concern, not merely process it; and to recognise the human being behind the file.

That is the standard. That is the conclusion.

Preservation first. Examination second. Conclusion last.

No institutional immunity. No erasure of testimony. No human being reduced to a file.

Time is the witness. Truth is the judge.

The witness remains.
