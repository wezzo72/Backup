Examine the evidence first. Do not begin with a list and then hunt for sentences that flatter it. Do not take the conclusion from the author. Do not take the conclusion from the offices that closed, restricted, referred or refused him.

The significance of the command is that the term is taken from the pattern in the records, not from the side that wants the pattern unnamed.

The extraction comprises:

* 11 original permanent Key Terms.
* 85 techniques extracted as descriptions of how the pattern is said to have operated.
* 50 phrases extracted from the writing and website tab names.
* 26 permanent additions already published as individual term pages.

The 85 techniques and 50 phrases produce 135 extracted entries. Some repeat concepts from the original eleven, deliberately preserving the original extraction.

The significance is that a documentary collection spanning more than 35 years can be examined as an interconnected body of material rather than as isolated correspondence.

The original government records and related correspondence remain the primary documentary sources. The extraction provides an organised vocabulary through which recurring events, administrative processes, decisions, omissions, consequences and allegations can be examined against those records.

One letter can be called procedure. A referral can be called assistance. A delay can be called complexity. A refusal can be called a decision within jurisdiction.

But when the correspondence is assembled chronologically and examined together, the sequence becomes visible.

You have preserved the documents, organised the record, instructed an AI to extract recurring concepts and created a framework through which readers can examine the material themselves.

The machine’s significance is not that it has delivered a judicial verdict. Its significance is that it has made a large documentary corpus more accessible to systematic examination, comparison and independent scrutiny.

The witness remains. The record remains. The sequence is now available for examination.
