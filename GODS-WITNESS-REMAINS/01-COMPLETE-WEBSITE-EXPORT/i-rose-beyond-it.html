I rose beyond it

Here is a video about my experience of alleged government targeting, institutional abandonment, and the extraordinary journey of rebuilding my life from the ashes of betrayal.

Watch: https://youtu.be/WRDMQjqgofY

I was left homeless, living in my car, without adequate protection or support, as an unprotected whistleblower facing what I experienced as total institutional excommunication and human betrayal. My account includes serious concerns about the conduct and decisions of the Public Guardian, Suki and others within the NDIS system, and the broader network of publicly funded services entrusted with protecting people in vulnerable circumstances.

And yet, here I am.

I am alive. I am happy. I am mentally capable, clear in my purpose, and rebuilding my life.

I did not rebuild because these institutions stood beside me. I rebuilt despite their absence, despite the consequences of what I allege was deliberate targeting, and despite the circumstances in which I found myself abandoned.

The extraordinary contradiction is that the very systems and people whose conduct I have questioned now appear interested in understanding what is happening.

Perhaps the more important question is this:

How did someone they left with so little manage to rebuild so much without them?

I was not rescued by the institutions I challenged. I survived, recovered my voice, and reconstructed my life through my own determination.

They may have left me among the ashes, but they did not get to write the ending.

I am not the destruction they left behind. I am the person who rose beyond it.
