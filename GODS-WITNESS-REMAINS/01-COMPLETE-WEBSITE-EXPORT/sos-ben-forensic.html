On the SOS · 3 November 2026 · His words · The messages set out · Not a police finding

The political chronology, the NDIS, Ablepoint and the unanswered questions of accountability

I have published an SOS seeking assistance to escape what I describe as an entrapment scenario involving Ablepoint, an NDIS provider in New South Wales. Despite publicly expressing my wish to leave, I remain in circumstances that I describe as abusive, neglectful, financially oppressive and unsafe. I have repeatedly sought assistance and accountability, yet I remain concerned that the systems responsible for protecting me have failed to respond adequately.

My concerns extend to the political and ministerial responsibility of Bill Shorten, whom I identify as a central figure in my allegations of abuse and institutional betrayal. I allege that Shorten used, or sought to use, his considerable political influence against me. These are serious allegations that warrant examination against documentary evidence, official correspondence, dates and decisions.

The political chronology begins in May 2019, when Bill Shorten, then Leader of the Australian Labor Party, lost the federal election to Scott Morrison. Anthony Albanese subsequently became Labor leader on 30 May 2019. On 21 May 2022, Labor won the federal election, and Anthony Albanese became Prime Minister. On 1 June 2022, Bill Shorten was appointed Minister for the NDIS and Minister for Government Services.

This appointment is particularly relevant because the NDIS was the very system through which I was seeking protection, assistance and support. Shorten held ministerial responsibility for the portfolio during a period in which I was raising concerns about my treatment, disability support, financial circumstances and the conduct of service providers. I question whether my petitions, complaints and correspondence were properly received, considered, referred and answered during this period, and whether records establish direct involvement by his office.

I also question the chronology of my petitions in relation to Shorten’s political career, his failure to become Prime Minister in 2019 and his subsequent ministerial appointment under Albanese in 2022. I want the dates, petition identification numbers, recipients, ministerial correspondence and departmental responses examined to determine whether any documentary relationship exists.

The parliamentary petition identification remains an outstanding documentary question in this sense: a Change.org petition or a committee submission must not be misrepresented as an Australian Parliament e-petition. My archive identifies a petition titled Sign the Petition — End the Systemic Torture of Barran Dodger and a document titled Question for MP Bill Shorten Concerning the NDIS. The mailbox on this site also records a House of Representatives screen. The forensic account of that number is set out below, and it is not invented.

My allegations concerning Shorten also include the involvement of a person I have identified as Hood Mirabi, whom I allege was presented as someone who could assist me despite my concerns about his identity, qualifications and role. I recall being told anonymously that this person would, in words attributed to the speaker, “erase me”. I question how that person could have known about my communications with Hood Mirabi and whether there is any independently verifiable connection between those events. I further allege that Shorten was involved in circumstances surrounding a police arrest on a charge I maintain was false. I want the police records, grounds for arrest and subsequent court documentation examined.

A magistrate subsequently made an Apprehended Violence Order against me, positioning me as the person against whom protective measures were required. I dispute the characterisation of myself as the threat and question the circumstances, evidence and representations that resulted in the order. I believe the proceedings must be considered alongside my earlier complaints, my attempts to obtain protection and the circumstances in which I say I was being subjected to abuse and intimidation.

My concerns also include allegations of sexual misconduct that I say were falsely attributed to me. I maintain that the relevant circumstances were consensual and that information communicated to Ben by Federal Police confirmed this distinction. I want the original police records and any official findings examined rather than relying on competing accounts or public characterisations.

The Ben NDIS messages. I regard them as literal proof.

I regard what Ben wrote as literal proof of the assassination attempt. I want that stated, and I want the words themselves, not a summary. An important part of my documentary account concerns text messages involving Ben, an individual I identify in connection with my NDIS support circumstances. I have preserved these communications under the titles Ben-NDIS-Help and Ben-Ndis-Help-large. They are on this site at Ben’s messages and in the PDF.

In my published account of the messages, Ben described the alleged assassination attempt on my life as a “close call”. I also attribute to him an expression of fear that the people involved could “put a hit on me too”. Another statement attributed to Ben was that the corruption I was exposing “goes all the way to the top”. I consider these statements significant because they were communications from someone within my NDIS support environment, not merely my own later interpretation.

The significance I attach to Ben’s messages is that they record his own concern and his fear of consequences for himself. I regard this as corroboration, and I regard his acknowledgement as literal documentary proof of what he knew and wrote about the attempt. The exact wording, the pages, and the images are set out in the exhibit below. Ben’s statements do not name the person who gave an order. I want the police record that corresponds to the close call produced. I am not withdrawing the proof I say the messages are. I am demanding the police file beside them.

I have also described communications in which Ben relayed information from Federal Police concerning a sexual experience from my earlier life. According to my account, the information conveyed to him was that the experience was regrettable but consensual. I want the original Federal Police communication, if it exists, and the exact messages examined.

A further concern is my account that Ben was subsequently required to sign a non-disclosure agreement and was instructed not to discuss the matter. I have described this as an agreement imposed following an alleged Federal Police or government intervention. I want the actual agreement, its author, date, legal basis and circumstances of execution established. I also want to know whether Ben was told that disclosure could expose him to federal charges, including the reference to treason that appears in my published account.

I have previously described concerns about information being removed from his phone and about restrictions on what he could communicate. The existence of an NDA must be tested against the original document. What is already fixed is what he wrote about it.

I do not want these communications reduced to a general statement that I felt threatened. Each message is set out below with the page it is published on. The reader can distinguish what Ben wrote, what he reported being told by police, and what I understand those words to prove.

The petition, in forensic detail

The documents named in the archive are: Sign the Petition — End the Systemic Torture of Barran Dodger, and Question for MP Bill Shorten Concerning the NDIS.

The mailbox records a further, specific fact. On 13 July 2022 a letter addressed “Dear Mr Shorten” was sent, and under it the House of Representatives screen is pasted: petition created, verification succeeded, e-petition number EN4358. The day is set out at 13 July 2022 and on the minister. The public page that would carry the number is EN4358. It does not display the petition. No later letter in that mailbox from the Petitions Committee says EN4358 was certified, and no later letter says which rule it failed.

That is the evidence of the petition that is on this record. EN4358 is the number the House screen in the mailbox gives. It is not a number invented on this page. A Change.org page is not that e-petition. The question that remains is what the Petitions Committee did with EN4358 after the number was issued.

Ablepoint, and the questions that remain

I allege that I have been subjected to abuse, neglect, surveillance, intimidation and financial hardship while remaining with Ablepoint. I have publicly stated that I wish to leave. On 20 January 2025 Bill Shorten left the ministry. His departure did not end my circumstances. Whether Ablepoint Australia is the entity responsible for my care, and whether more than one ABN was used, is examined on the compliance record. That examination is on this SOS page with this statement.

I have survived what I describe as almost total human betrayal. I have spoken about homelessness in New South Wales, living in my car with my therapy dog, Crystal. I hold a PhD. My qualifications do not make the allegations automatically correct, and political office does not make anyone immune from scrutiny.

My objective is not revenge. It is the truth, the preservation of evidence, personal safety, freedom from circumstances I describe as abusive, and accountability wherever the evidence establishes that responsibility exists.

Did my petitions reach the offices? Were the complaints about Ablepoint investigated? What evidence supported the police action and the AVO? What do Ben’s original messages establish? Was he required to sign a confidentiality agreement, and by whom? Is there a police record of the close call? Were public funds expended? Why do I remain where I have publicly asked to leave?
