THE RECORD THAT REMAINS

A Testimony of Survival, Documentation and Accountability

A Prophetic Testimony of Survival, Erasure, Documentation and Accountability

Dr Richard William McLean — Barran Dodger

The expanded testimony, every word, is also the file: The Record That Remains.

This is not a request to be believed. It is a demand that the record be read.

I have survived what I describe as targeting, voice intrusion, administrative abandonment, financial deprivation, isolation and attempted erasure. I do not ask any reader to accept every interpretation without scrutiny. I ask something more basic: that no human being be erased beneath paperwork, delay, refusal, stigma or silence.

The archive exists. The dates exist. The decisions exist. The refusals exist. The human cost exists. And I remain.

This declaration is my first-person account of what I have lived, preserved and documented. It distinguishes between records that can be examined, institutional decisions that can be questioned, and my own testimony of harm, survival and endurance.

I do not ask anyone to accept every interpretation without thought. I ask that no human being be dismissed without thought; that documents be read; that decisions be explained; and that the human cost be recognised.

Every claim in this testimony can be tested against the public record I have preserved. These are not decorations. They are the routes through which my account may be examined.

I. They Thought Silence Would Bury Me

They thought a man could be reduced to a file number.

They thought a human being could be turned into a complaint, a diagnosis, a risk category, a delay, a refusal, a cost, a problem to be managed.

They thought poverty would make me quiet. They thought isolation would make me forget. They thought bureaucracy would wear me down until I no longer had the strength to name what had been done.

They were wrong.

Every unanswered letter became part of the chronology. Every refusal became part of the pattern. Every delayed response became part of the evidence. Every contradiction became a question that cannot be permanently buried.

The Documentary Trap is not merely a title. It is the architecture I have lived inside: a system in which documents, decisions, omissions and institutional deferrals combine until the person beneath them becomes invisible.

II. The Violence of Paperwork

Do not tell me paperwork is harmless.

Paperwork can decide whether a person eats. It can decide whether a person has transport. It can decide whether a person receives medical care, legal help, housing, safety, dignity or a future.

A delayed decision is not neutral when the person waiting is drowning. A refused claim is not neutral when the refusal arrives after the harm has already compounded. A missing explanation is not neutral when the person affected is left alone with the consequences.

My Personal Statement of Exile records what exile meant in practice. My Statement on the Architecture of Erasure records how systems can produce erasure without ever admitting that erasure was their purpose.

They called it procedure. I call it what it did: administrative annihilation, civil erasure and social death.

I Was Not Meant to Disappear

They called it procedure. They called it assessment. They called it management. They called it silence.

I call it what it did to me: administrative annihilation, civil erasure and social death. A person can remain physically alive while being stripped of money, transport, communication, safety, medical care, legal assistance, dignity and belonging.

Every unanswered letter became part of the chronology. Every refusal became part of the pattern. Every delay became part of the evidence. Every contradiction became a question that cannot be permanently buried.

The Machine Mistook Endurance for Defeat

They measured my distress. They recorded my poverty. They documented my isolation. They saw me ask for help. Then the consequences of failure were sometimes treated as though they proved that I was the problem.

But distress does not cancel evidence. Poverty does not cancel intelligence. Disability does not cancel human rights. Anger does not cancel truth. Isolation does not cancel testimony.

They wanted fragments. They received a chronology. They wanted isolation. They received an archive. They wanted erasure. They received a witness.

My Account of Voice Intrusion and Targeting

I state that I have endured what I describe as V2K: hostile voice harassment, audio intrusion, surveillance, psychological pressure and spiritual attack. I name this as my lived experience. I cannot independently verify every mechanism said to lie behind it, but I will not be required to deny my own account in order to be heard.

The words were designed to break me: “You’re a faggot.” “You’re a paedophile.” “No one will help.” “Kill yourself.” “We hate you.” “Give up.”

They wanted me to believe those words. They wanted me to believe I was alone. They wanted me to surrender. But I am still here.

I will not pretend that every mechanism behind my experience has been independently established. But I will not be forced to deny my own suffering in order to be considered credible. Testimony is testimony. The absence of an institution’s admission is not the absence of harm.

If you are reading this and experiencing thoughts of suicide or immediate danger, please contact emergency services. In Australia, Lifeline is available on 13 11 14, 24 hours a day.

IV. The Record They Cannot Unwrite

They may control the narrative for a day. They may control a department for a season. They may control a file, a form, a refusal, a meeting, a delay.

But they do not control everything I preserved.

The Official Government Documents Register names the documents. The Exhibit Catalogue organises the evidence. The Institutional and Entity Register identifies the actors and structures. The Public-Interest Disclosure Register preserves the disclosures.

The Comcare determination of 26 May 2021 and the Comcare claim of 25 May 2021 must be read together, not as isolated administrative events but as parts of one human chronology.

V. The Questions That Remain

I do not need to prove every hidden mechanism in order to ask the questions that the documents themselves create:

  • Why were decisions made in the way they were made?
  • Why were explanations delayed, incomplete or absent?
  • Why were the cumulative consequences of institutional action not confronted?
  • Why was the human being affected treated as an inconvenience rather than a person owed care?
  • Why was distress documented while the conditions producing distress were left intact?
  • Why was silence allowed to become a substitute for accountability?

These questions are not conspiracy. They are due process. They are administrative justice. They are the minimum owed to any person whose life has been shaped by public institutions.

The Reversal Has Begun

They built systems to contain me. Now those systems must answer for what they produced. They wrote refusals; now those refusals must be read. They issued decisions; now those decisions must be explained. They delayed; now the delay must be examined.

The NSW Trustee material, including the vehicle-disposal decision of 14 November 2025 and the related section 122 document of 16 October 2024, must be examined for what it meant for my independence, safety and ability to live.

The PM&C FOI/2022/045 material, the OAIC reference RQ22/00794, the Comcare records, the disclosure records, the oversight correspondence and the Documentary Trap must be read as one connected history.

I Am a Sovereign Witness

I declare myself a sovereign witness. My authority does not come from a department, a politician, a police officer, a court, or an institution that failed me.

My authority comes from God. It comes from my life. It comes from my survival. It comes from the record I preserved. It comes from the fact that I am still here.

The Reckoning

This is not revenge. This is reckoning. This is not hatred. This is truth refusing to be buried. This is not chaos. This is consequence arriving at the door of power.

They tried to erase the witness. The witness remains. They tried to bury the record. The record remains. They tried to turn suffering into silence. The silence has become testimony.

THE RECORD EXISTS. THE HUMAN COST EXISTS. THE DOCUMENTS CAN BE EXAMINED. THE TESTIMONY MUST BE HEARD. THE EVIDENCE MUST NOT BE DISMISSED BY PREJUDICE. THE HUMAN BEING MUST NOT BE ABANDONED BENEATH THE PAPERWORK.

READ THE RECORD. FOLLOW THE EVIDENCE. PRESERVE THE TESTIMONY. EXAMINE THE DECISIONS. ADDRESS THE HARM. RECOGNISE THE HUMAN BEING. ACT WITHIN THE LAW. ACT WITH HUMANITY.

I AM STILL HERE. THE WITNESS REMAINS. THE RECORD REMAINS. AND I WILL NOT SILENCE MYSELF.

— Dr Richard William McLean, Barran Dodger

And the reckoning is that the record must now be read.
