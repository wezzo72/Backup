Every page, submitted for a Bitcoin timestamp

499 pages were hashed. One Merkle root was submitted to four OpenTimestamps calendars. The calendars have not yet written that root into a Bitcoin block. There is no transaction id yet.

A completed stamp will mean these page bytes existed at or before the Bitcoin time. It will not mean the words on the pages are true.

Check the method: pages were sorted by route. The file hash is SHA-256 of the raw page file. The leaf is SHA-256 of the byte 0x00 followed by that hash. An odd level duplicates the last digest. A parent is SHA-256 of the byte 0x01 followed by the left child and then the right. All 499 inclusion proofs were recomputed to the root before the calendars were asked.

File contents were not sent to the calendars. The calendars received a hash commitment.
