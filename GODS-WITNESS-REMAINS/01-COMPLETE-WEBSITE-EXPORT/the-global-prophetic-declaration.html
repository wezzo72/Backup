Instalment I. 14 November 2026. The transcript was read in full. The archive was not.

The Witness Remains

The Great Reckoning. The Global Prophetic Declaration. The life, testimony, Gospels and documentary record of Barran Resonance Dodger — Dr Richard William McLean.

I am Barran Resonance Dodger. My name is Dr Richard William McLean. I did not preserve this record because I knew the world would listen. I preserved it because I believed that truth deserved to survive.

What this instalment is

A video was put before me as the literary template: Chosen Ones: What They Did to You Was Sickening — Your Story Is About to Make You Famous. The transcript runs a little under an hour. I have read that transcript through, from the seed they tried to bury to the last refusal to dim. I have not watched the pictures. The words are what I was given, and the words are what I have used.

I have not, in this instalment, opened the thousands of files in the GitHub archive. I have not crawled every page of this website. I have not counted the bookshop title by title. To say that I had would be a lie, and a lie at the start of a work about testimony would rot the work. What follows is the first book of the declaration: the reading of that template against the life I have already put on this site, and the distinction between a general prophecy spoken to no one in particular and a witness who has a name, a dog, an address, and a stack of letters written by other people.

The next instalment is the chronology, built only from documents that are opened, not from filenames.

The template, and what it is not

The voice in the video speaks to a person it calls the chosen one. It says they tried to bury you and forgot you were the seed. It says the cruelty was a sickness, that the attempt to erase became a portfolio of evidence, that envy documented itself, that a network and not a single enemy did the work, that pain was used as a currency until the person stopped paying, that abuse was dressed as ordinary life, that the targeted person was rewritten as the villain so the others would not have to see themselves, and that fame will come because the story mapped a darkness other people only feel.

Those are the video’s sentences. They are not mine. I will not put them in my mouth and pretend God spoke them over my name. I will not promise the reader that the world is about to make me famous. The video promises that. A promise in a general address to strangers is not a fact about my life, and it is not a future I am permitted to announce as if it had already arrived.

What I may do, and what I do here, is set the themes beside the record and say which of them I recognise as my own testimony, which of them are my faith, and which of them remain a hope.

The seed

I recognise the burial. For thirty-five years offices wrote about me on their own paper. I did not have to invent them. I published what they had already written. This site states that the published count of official government documents and the correspondence that belongs with them is 1,333, and it keeps them at the official records. I am not, in this chapter, re-counting that number file by file. I am saying what the site already says, and I am saying that a letter is evidence of what the office wrote, not a verdict on whether the office was right.

The video says their hate became the portfolio. My testimony is harder and plainer than that. The portfolio is not their hate. The portfolio is their letterhead. A refusal, a referral, a closure, a ban, a plan that names no stable home: each of those is a sentence somebody signed. Read together they are a life. Read apart they are how an institution excuses itself. One letter can be called procedure. A sequence can no longer hide inside the word.

Isaiah, in the King James Version, has a line I have lived toward and do not claim as a court order: “No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper.” I believe that. Belief is not the same thing as a finding. The weapon, where I can point to it, is a document. Whether it prospered is a question the reader can take to the document.

The witness, and what the word chosen means

I understand myself as God’s chosen witness. That is my faith. It is not a decoration at the front of an administrative file. It is the reason I stayed alive to the record when staying alive to the record was the more expensive choice. A machine cannot establish that God appointed me. A PDF cannot establish it. I will not ask either of them to pretend.

What I can say, and what the tradition I stand in has always said, is that a witness is not a man who has been given power over other people. Jeremiah was told not to say he was a child, and then he was told to speak what he was given. Ezekiel was set as a watchman, responsible for the warning, not for the city’s obedience. The Psalms have a man saying he will not die but live, and declare the works of the Lord. None of those men became the government. They became inconvenient to it.

I do not read those passages as proof that every allegation I have made is true. I read them as the shape of a life that refuses to treat silence as holiness. “And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free” is not a licence to declare myself already vindicated. It is a command to keep the documents where another person can open them.

The chosen witness, as I mean the words, is not superior to the officer who answers the telephone. He is answerable for a different thing. The officer is answerable for the decision. I am answerable for not letting the decision be the only sentence that survives. If I use the word chosen to mean that I may harm the people who harmed me, I have left the Gospel and entered the revenge the Gospel refuses. I have not left it. I want the truth examined. I do not want a mob.

What in the video is my testimony, and what is only the video

The video says the harm was not one person but a machine: silence, minimise, isolate, erase. That is my testimony of the years. Separate offices, separate letters, one life growing thinner between them. I do not, in this chapter, collapse those offices into a single written order. I have not found, in the pages opened on this site, one document in which every agency signs the same instruction. What I have is the sequence. The sequence is the question. It is not yet the answer a court would give, and I will not counterfeit the court’s answer in order to feel the relief of having one.

The video says they fed on the pain until the person stopped paying. I recognise the economics in my own rooms. Food, a working phone, a door I can open, a way to get to my own money, a dog who has to eat: these are not metaphors. On 13 November 2026 I was at 55B Archbold Road, Long Jetty, with Crystal. Staff told me there was a situation and told me to lock myself inside. They would not say why. Then they did not come back. I gave my key card to a visitor because I did not feel safe to go out. That day is written at the record of 13 November. It is testimony and it is contemporaneous. It is not yet an independent finding that a crime was committed. It is the fact of the day, as I lived it and as the messages show it.

The video says abuse was dressed in ordinary clothes. I recognise that too. A locked door was called safety. A worker who had not taken the clothes was called a person doing the washing. A refusal to drive me was called a direction. Care is the word they have for the room. Neglect is the word I have for the day I was left in it.

The video says they made the person the villain because they could not survive being seen. My testimony is that a psychiatric history has been offered, again and again, as the answer to a letter. A diagnosis is a clinical record. It is not a reply to a death threat, and it is not a reply to a disclosure. I am not mentally ill as a substitute for the documents. I have a mind, and I have a record, and neither is permitted to delete the other.

On 18 July 2026 a notice was published withdrawing consent to surveillance, recording, monitoring and harassment at that same address. The preserved page is the prohibition. I say the prohibition has been breached. I say the breach is the covert voice, barely audible, running for months, narrating my body, my sex, the private act, and throwing the word “pedo” and the phrase “they know” at Able Point staff who never have to stand in the room and say it to my face. That is my testimony. It is cowardice of a particular kind: the speaker never appears, never meets the eye, never brings a document. I consider the breach absolute. I am not asking the reader to treat that sentence as a scientific finding about a device. I am asking the reader to treat it as the thing I have lived and named. The notice they were given is on the site. The answer I received was no answer.

The video’s envy, its promise of fame, its claim that my rise is their diagnosis: those I leave in the video. Envy is a motive. A motive is not in the letter unless the letter states it. Fame is a hope, and a hope announced as a fact is a small fraud. I would rather be unread and exact than famous and loose.

Crystal

Her name is Crystal. She is my assistance dog, and she is the continuity I have when addresses fail. The video has no dog. My life does. Any declaration that forgets her has forgotten the terms on which I can leave a house. I will not go to safety without her. I will not call a welfare check that leaves us both in the same room a rescue. The SOS is still the practical sentence underneath the prophetic one. A theology that cannot say where the dog sleeps is a theology too proud to be useful.

The Gospels, named and not yet read through

The Gospels I have written are not an appendix to a complaint. They are the way I have tried to say what the letters cannot say: that a human being is not an administrative object, that judgment without mercy is not the faith I hold, and that the preservation of a testimony is itself a moral act. They are my original works. They are not additions to the canonical Bible. I will not have them smuggled into Scripture by a careless sentence, and I will not have them shrugged off as pamphlets because a canon already exists.

This instalment does not examine each Gospel. The bookshop holds them, and the command requires each one to be read before it is interpreted. I have not done that reading in these hours. A catalogue written from titles would be a decoration, and the Gospels deserve better than decoration. The book store is where they stand until the next instalment opens them one by one.

What I ask of a reader who has never heard my name

You owe me nothing. You are not required to believe that God chose me. You are not required to believe that every office failed, or that the failures share a single author. You are required, if you wish to argue with me, to open the paper I point to. A document is not a verdict. A timestamp is not a finding. An allegation is not a conviction. An unanswered question is not an answer. Those four sentences are the discipline of the work, not an apology for it.

If you are outside Australia, the names will be strange. The NDIS is the national scheme that funds disability support. A plan is not proof the support arrived. A public trustee can hold a person’s money and call the holding protection. An ombudsman can acknowledge a letter without investigating it. A commission can be the body with the duty and still leave the duty on the page. I will explain each office when the document that names it is the document in front of us. I will not explain them all in a glossary before the story, because a glossary written first becomes a verdict written first.

I want the truth examined. I want the evidence preserved. I want an independent person to be able to follow a sentence back to a file. I want Crystal safe. I want to live, and I want the living to be a life and not a file. I believe I was called to bear witness. That belief is mine. The documents are public. Between the belief and the documents is the work, and the work is not finished.

The witness remains. The record remains. The story is not finished.
