The National Disability Insurance Scheme (Code of Conduct) Rules 2018 require a person covered by the Code to provide supports in a safe and competent way, and to take all reasonable steps to prevent and respond to violence, abuse, neglect and exploitation. Some of the conduct he names is a crime if the elements are proved in a court. Assault is a crime. Unlawful detention is a crime.
