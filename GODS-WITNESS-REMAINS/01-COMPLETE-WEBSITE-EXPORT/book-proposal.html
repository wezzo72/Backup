Book proposal: The Great Reckoning

Title. The Great Reckoning.

Subtitle. Thirty-five years of records, and the man who would not let them disappear.

Author. Dr Richard William McLean, Barran Resonance Dodger. Earlier books: Recovered, Not Cured (2002), and Betrayed, Murdered, Forsaken (Apple Books id6618112290). The Manifesto of the Unerased is a title on this site whose definitive public manuscript link is not confirmed. See the books page.

Why it matters

A disabled whistleblower kept the letters. The letters show referrals, decisions and silences. He says the human result was exile, poverty and a life that institutions found easier to move on from than to answer. The book would let a reader watch a document and a life in the same chapter, and would say which is which.

The narrative

Beginning: a journalist, artist and advocate, and the book he published in 2002. Middle: the offices, the NDIS, the Trustee, the legal-aid doors, the 2021 hospital record he publishes, the message about Bill Shorten, the 2023 Attorney-General’s letter. End: he is still here, with Crystal, publishing the archive and asking to be read. Faith is a chapter of its own, not a footnote that proves the facts.

Sources

The GitHub archive, the chronology, the government register, and the pages named in the briefing. Interviews would require consent. No institution has agreed to be interviewed.

Audience and comparables

Readers of Australian investigative nonfiction and disability memoir. No comparable title is claimed as a sibling of this book, because none was checked as a match in this pass.

What a publisher would still need

A chapter outline locked to documents, a word count, a sample chapter, and a decision about what medical detail stays in a trade book. None of that has been delivered to a publishing house. No advance has been offered.
