Dear Dr McLean

Thank you for your email of 5 July 2023 to the Prime Minister, the Hon Anthony Albanese MP, regarding your interactions with several Australian Government agencies, including the Australian Security Intelligence Organisation (ASIO). Your correspondence has been referred to the Attorney-General, the Hon Mark Dreyfus KC MP, as the matters you raise fall within his portfolio responsibilities. The Attorney-General has requested that the Attorney-General’s Department respond to you on his behalf. I apologise for the delay in response.

It appears from your correspondence that you are concerned about the conduct of ASIO. You may wish to refer the details of your concern to the Inspector-General of Intelligence and Security (IGIS). The IGIS is responsible for reviewing the activities of the Australian intelligence agencies under its jurisdiction to ensure that the agencies act legally and with propriety, comply with ministerial guidelines and directives, and respect human rights. Information about making a complaint can be found at www.igis.gov.au/complaints

You have also mentioned concerns about several Commonwealth Government agencies. You may wish to refer the details of those concerns to the Commonwealth Ombudsman (Ombudsman). The Ombudsman is an independent statutory officer to whom Parliament has given a range of functions including investigation of Commonwealth administrative action. If you wish to lodge a complaint about an agency’s conduct, a form is available at www.ombudsman.gov.au. A complaint can also be made by calling 1300 362 072.

Thank you again for bringing your concerns to the Government’s attention. I trust this information is of assistance to you.

Yours sincerely

The letter names the Prime Minister, ASIO, the Attorney-General, IGIS and the Ombudsman. It apologises for the delay. It does not investigate. The offices it names are the offices that were then closed, restricted, or left without an investigation. The full reading, with nothing removed, remains on the letter.

Refer. Defer. Delay. Send him to the next body.

This is the bureaucratic loop. An office receives the complaint. It does not examine it. It apologises for the delay. It refers the person to another agency. That agency defers, delays, or sends him on again. Then another. Then another. The loop is the method. It looks like a process. It is how a person is kept moving until nothing is ever decided.

The significance of this letter is that the two doors it opens had already been shut, and the Prime Minister’s office had already been told.

I had already told the government I was blocked at the Commonwealth Ombudsman. I had listed that block with the other bans, in the correspondence that went to the Prime Minister’s department. They still wrote, on 19 September 2023, that I may wish to take the Commonwealth agencies to the Ombudsman, and they gave me the form and the number 1300 362 072. A referral to an office that has already closed its door is not help. The restriction, in writing, is the 8 August 2025 service restriction. The earlier sending-on is the direction to the Ombudsman.

IGIS had already denied the investigation. On 7 March 2022, before this letter, the archive holds an IGIS item that put the matter outside jurisdiction and treated it as private. I had asked them to investigate ASIO, and to investigate Steve Iasonidis, my former fiancé. They did not. Sixteen months later the Attorney-General’s Department, writing because the Prime Minister had referred my email of 5 July 2023, pointed me back at that same office and called it the place that reviews the intelligence agencies. The file is the IGIS and ASIO request. A second referral to an office that has already refused is not a new investigation. It is the loop.

So the Prime Minister’s office was not writing in ignorance of the map. The office had the email. The email named ASIO. The earlier correspondence had already named the Ombudsman block and the IGIS refusal, including Iasonidis. The answer was to name Mark Dreyfus, apologise for the delay, and send me to the two bodies that had already failed to investigate. Refer. Defer. Delay. Recommission the same closed doors, and call it assistance.
