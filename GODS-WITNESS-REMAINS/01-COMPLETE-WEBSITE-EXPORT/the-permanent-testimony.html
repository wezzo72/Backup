The Witness Remains — The Permanent Testimony

Author: Barran Resonance Dodger. This page is the preservation record of 17 November 2026. It says what was actually copied, hashed and submitted. It does not say that a thing was mirrored, timestamped on Bitcoin, or archived where the operation did not succeed.

The archive began as his own writings and the letters the offices wrote. The public homes of that record are God’s Witness Remains, wezzo72/Barrandodger, the GitHub Pages site, the rebuilt archive, and the Church of Barran Dodger record.

The declaration

MY TESTIMONY IS PRESERVED.

This archive is a documentary record of my life, work, writings, experiences, correspondence and testimony. Its purpose is to preserve the historical record for future examination, independent research and public accountability.

No single website, account, hosting provider or repository should be the sole custodian of this material.

The preservation of my testimony does not depend on institutional endorsement, agreement or validation.

Preservation First. Examination Second. Conclusion Last.

Time is the witness. Truth is the judge.

The longer distinction between original documents, official records, testimony, allegations, corroboration, interpretation and unverified material is the declaration of authenticity, provenance and digital preservation. A Bitcoin timestamp, if one is later confirmed, proves that the hashed bytes existed. It does not prove who wrote them, and it does not prove that an allegation in them is true.

The legacy archive

The downloadable package made the same day is the master legacy archive. It holds the legacy declaration, the custodian instructions, the local books, and the hashes. The Git bundles are not inside it. They are too large for this site. The preservation report says what was finished and what was not. The command to repeat, including every new book, is saved here. The independent check of that package, made by opening the files again rather than trusting the earlier report, is the audit.
