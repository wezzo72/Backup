His own published religious work takes that register and makes it his. It is not evidence that the events occurred. It is evidence that this is the meaning he has given them, and it is a body of work in its own right. The library is the Gospel Library. The long impartial narrative is The Gospel of the Witness Remains. The declaration of dignity is The Gospel of Sovereignty. The examination of wealth and office is The Power They Cannot Buy. The paper that takes the same video’s cadence into the wider record is the prophetic declaration of 16 November. The mother, the family, and the sixteen hospitalisations are the reckoning of the mother.

The Holy Reckoning, as this examination uses the phrase, is not revenge and not a claim that God has been interviewed. It is the demand that a public power account for a public record, without a predetermined sentence, and without the person’s disability, poverty, diagnosis or faith being used as a reason not to read. Accountability without revenge. Truth without fabrication. Justice without prejudice. Humanity without exception. The video’s glory is a religious hope. The documentary equivalent of glory, on the papers read, would be narrower and harder: a letter that answers the question that was asked, a house, a meal, a dog fed, a file produced or refused in a sentence a reader can check. None of the offices whose letters were opened has said, in public, “we were wrong about him.” The bank said it was wrong about one lockout. That is the whole of the recantation in the set that was read.

Science, surveillance, and what a letter cannot settle

He publishes claims about covert electronic harassment, barely audible narration, and technologies he treats as a weapon. Those claims sit in his essays and in the terms he has asked to be examined, including the page linked from the record as V2K and surveillance. A theological or political narrative cannot establish a physical mechanism. A physical mechanism, if it is to be established, needs the device, the measurement, or the expert report. None of those was opened as a laboratory record in this pass. The claims are not deleted. They are not promoted into findings. The same rule that stops an office from being believed merely because it is an office stops a technological claim from being believed merely because it is his.

What the letters do settle, without any theory of a device, is the administrative sequence. A complaint closed. A disclosure not allocated. An email filtered. A premises ban. An estate put under management. A vehicle disposed of. A housing support refused. A plan that records no stable accommodation while approving $366,406.69. A bank error and a payment to end the complaint. A police information release that does not let the assault be read. An injury file whose title says fatal and revived, and whose opened text is an email, not a chart. That sequence does not need a hidden transmitter to be a sequence. It needs to be read in order.

What the fourteen movements come to

The video moves from silence, to a ruined name, to the mockery of a decision, to innocence as a mirror, to a camera that was always on, to the examination reversed, to one person made to carry the rest, to a cashed reputation, to a scale, to a future self, to restraint mistaken for weakness, to delay, to silence as the exhibit, to a name rewritten, to a demand that the correction be public.

The record answers in the same order, and it answers smaller. The silence is the unanswered ask. The ruined name is the allegation of a paid discrediting and a close call, printed and not yet matched to a police file. The mockery of a decision is the loop of referral, from Calder’s enquiry to a closed Victorian file, from PID to PID, from the Prime Minister’s email to Riley’s two addresses. The mirror is the Commission saying it cannot confer the status, and the man publishing the letter that says so. The camera, in documents, is not a divine recording. It is the fact that the offices wrote the letters and he kept them. The reversed examination is this page: the institutions’ sentences set out where a prosecution of him is not. The person made to carry the rest is the man with no stable accommodation on a plan worth $366,406.69, asking for food. The cashed reputation is not a proved pawnshop. It is a bank’s $500 to close a complaint, and a reported message that someone was paid to discredit him. The scale was not shown to be a strategy. It was shown to be a file that changed its own story. The future self is the publication, not a prophecy. The restraint is his own police letter, which accuses and then refuses to ask for a finding. The delay is the dates, from February 2022 to November 2026, without a decision on the conduct. The silence as exhibit is the paper. The name rewritten is the refusal that still carries the name. The public correction has not been spoken by the offices. It has been spoken by the publication of their sentences.

That is enough to justify the work. It is not enough to justify a sentence of guilt against any named person. The Holy Reckoning, if it is to remain holy in the only sense this examination will use, does not get to skip the document on the way to the punishment.
