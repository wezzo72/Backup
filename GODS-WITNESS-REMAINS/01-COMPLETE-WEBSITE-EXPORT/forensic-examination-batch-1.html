The letter that was read on the page

On the government-record page, and again on the evidence-archive page, a letter is reproduced as preserved text. It is headed MC23-028244, 19 September 2023, A Riley, Security Law Section. This batch read that reproduction. It did not compare the reproduction with a signed PDF.

In the reproduction, the Department thanks Dr Richard McLean for an email of 5 July 2023 to the Prime Minister, the Hon Anthony Albanese MP, about interactions with several Australian Government agencies, including ASIO. It says the correspondence was referred to the Attorney-General, the Hon Mark Dreyfus KC MP, and that the Department is responding on his behalf. It apologises for the delay. It says a concern about the conduct of ASIO may be referred to the Inspector-General of Intelligence and Security, and that concerns about Commonwealth agencies may be referred to the Commonwealth Ombudsman. It gives those offices’ public complaint routes. It closes “Yours sincerely, A Riley”.

That is what the reproduced letter says. It is a referral of the correspondent to two other offices. It is not, in the text read, a statement that an investigation was carried out, and it is not a finding about the conduct complained of.

The page then adds its own sentence: the letter does not investigate, and the offices it names were then closed, restricted, or left without an investigation. That sentence is the page’s gloss. It is not a sentence of the letter as read here. This batch did not open the later IGIS or Ombudsman files, so it does not adopt the gloss as a fact.

The register’s own contradiction

The Official Government Documents Register says, in its prose, “802 register entries. Each one is below.” The anchors actually present on the stored page are OGDR-001 through OGDR-395, and there is no gap inside that run. Three hundred and ninety-five is not 802. This batch records both figures. It does not invent the missing numbers, and it does not treat 395 anchors as 395 documents read. The entries were not read one by one.

The same page sets out a scale: A, directly established documentary fact, limited to what the inspected pages say; B, corroborated claim; C, documented allegation or reported experience; D, reasoned inference; E, speculation, which the page says it avoids. That scale belongs to that register. This batch did not re-grade its entries.

The page also says no blockchain timestamp was re-checked for that register, and that a Git blob SHA is not a SHA-256 hash. This batch did not re-check one either.

The independence test, for this batch only

  • Would the same pattern appear if the author’s name were concealed? The counts would. They are counts of elements in stored pages. No pattern of misconduct is declared from them.
  • Would the same conclusion be reached if the documents concerned someone else? No conclusion about a person’s conduct is reached in this batch. The inventory method would be the same.
  • Has evidence that contradicts the author been tested? Only where an opened page itself states a limit. The underlying contrary files were not opened.
  • Has an institution’s explanation been tested against its original? The reproduced MC23-028244 text was read. It was not checked against a departmental PDF. Later institutional files were not opened.
  • Has an event been separated from an inference about purpose? Yes. A referral letter is recorded as a referral. The page’s sentence about offices later being closed is not adopted.
  • Have similar outcomes been separated from coordination? Yes. No coordination is inferred from the fact that many rows exist.
  • Have ordinary administrative explanations been left open? Yes. The letter’s own explanation is portfolio referral and an apology for delay. That explanation is not rejected and not confirmed beyond the text.
  • What weakens the interpretations already printed on the site? They were not re-proved against the PDFs. A prior page’s “justified” is not this batch’s finding.
  • Has mental-health history, disability, or faith been used as proof? No.
  • Can another person reproduce this batch? Yes, for the counts. Open the stored pages, count gov-row, data-year, evidence-row, and e-OGDR- anchors, and read the passages named above. They cannot reproduce a finished glossary from this batch, because this batch does not contain one.
