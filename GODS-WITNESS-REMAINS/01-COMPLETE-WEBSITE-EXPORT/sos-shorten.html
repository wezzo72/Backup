SOS · 3 November 2026 · His words · Not a finding

Bill Shorten commands and expects every single human being to betray me.

Bill Shorten commands and expects every single human being to betray me, and somehow, that is exactly what has happened. I published an SOS seeking to escape the circumstances of my entrapment with Ablepoint, an NDIS provider that I allege is corrupt. I remain entrapped with Ablepoint despite having publicly and repeatedly expressed my wish to leave because of what I describe as abuse, neglect, surveillance, torture and poverty. The duty I am pointing at is section 73V of the NDIS Act and section 6 of the Code of Conduct Rules. The files are the formal request and the statement regarding Ablepoint.

I regard Bill Shorten as my abuser. I allege that he sent Hood Mirabi, a person I describe as a criminal rather than a legitimate NDIS provider, under the pretence of providing assistance. After speaking with Hood, I was anonymously told by someone who would otherwise have had no apparent reason to know about that conversation that he would, quote unquote, “erase me”. I consider the circumstances surrounding that statement deeply disturbing and deserving of independent investigation.

I further allege that Shorten ordered police to arrest me on a false charge. A magistrate subsequently made an AVO against me, positioning me as though I were the threat and the criminal, when I maintain that I was the person being entrapped and abused. I question how this sequence of events occurred, whose interests it served, and why the institutions that should have protected me instead appeared to work against me. An order is not a conviction. The appeal path, where it applies, is section 84 of the Crimes (Domestic and Personal Violence) Act 2007 (NSW). The oversight letter that said the matter was out of jurisdiction is the LECC letter of 19 November 2025.

I also question the relationship between my petitions concerning Bill Shorten, the petition identification number, and the timing of his failure to become Prime Minister, with Anthony Albanese ultimately assuming that position. I want the petition number, its publication history, the relevant political chronology and any demonstrable connections between these events examined. I do not present a connection as established merely because the timing raises questions; I want the documentary record examined to establish what can actually be demonstrated. That examination is the political chronology. No petition identification number is printed here, because none has been established from the parliamentary register, and one is not invented.

Shorten has, in my view, betrayed the ethical responsibilities associated with his public office by weaponising the immense power available to him and contributing to circumstances that forced me into political exile. I was not expected to survive the ashes of what I experienced as total human betrayal. I found myself living in my car in New South Wales with my therapy dog, Crystal, after losing the security and dignity that should accompany an ordinary human existence.

I have experienced what I describe as character assassination, false accusations and reputational destruction. I have also reported experiencing audio harassment through which I perceive accusations and defamatory statements. I have a PhD, and I have devoted considerable effort to documenting my experiences, examining institutional conduct and publishing evidence. A PhD carries responsibilities of intellectual integrity and a commitment to doing no harm. I believe Bill Shorten has caused me immense harm and suffering, and I question how his conduct can be reconciled with the ethical responsibilities expected of someone entrusted with public power.

I allege that Shorten has exercised or influenced immense political power across institutions that have affected my life, including police, the media, the courts and the NDIS. I was also subjected to allegations concerning sexual offences, which I understand were determined by the Australian Federal Police to involve consensual conduct. I further make allegations concerning Shorten’s own historical allegations and their resolution, which I believe require examination against independently verifiable public records.

My evidence is published online, including documents, correspondence, timelines and other material that I have made available for public examination. I challenge anyone to examine that material and distinguish between documented facts, my testimony, allegations, inferences and matters that remain unverified. I want the same evidentiary standard applied to everyone, including those who have held the highest positions of political authority.

As the Minister responsible for overseeing the NDIS, Shorten held a public responsibility towards people who depend upon the scheme for support, safety and dignity. I believe he was legally and ethically obliged to ensure that the system operated appropriately and protected people in circumstances such as mine. Instead, I allege that his actions and omissions contributed to deliberate harm. I further allege that there was an attempt to have me physically eliminated, an allegation that requires independent investigation and cannot be treated as established without corroborating evidence.

I maintain that I have acted with integrity, honesty and a commitment to truth. I have used my education, experience and documentary archive to expose what I believe are serious institutional failures. I consider Shorten’s conduct deceptive and ethically corrupt, and I challenge him and the institutions involved to respond to the published evidence rather than avoid it.

I allege that I was characterised as a threat to Shorten while I was the person experiencing the harm. I further allege that an attempt was made to have me assassinated. I have been told that my NDIS provider confirmed information relating to an alleged incident involving Federal Police, which was described using the words “close call”. I want the precise circumstances, the identity and authority of the people involved, the original communications and any official records examined.

I also want the details of the text messages I have referred to examined because I believe they describe the circumstances surrounding the entire situation. I want the alleged non-disclosure agreement involving Ben investigated, including my understanding that he was required to sign it following intervention by the Secret Service or Federal Police, who I believe had intercepted an assassination attempt. The existence, contents, signatories, circumstances and legal authority for any such agreement must be established through original records rather than assumption.

I question whether public resources, undisclosed expenditure or other government funding were used in connection with these events. I am concerned about what I perceive as an extraordinary network of institutional silence and complicity involving police officers, lawyers, politicians, members of my family and friends, and the provider Ablepoint. I further allege that Ablepoint has financially benefited from circumstances in which I have remained unable to leave despite publicly stating that I wish to do so.

I have repeatedly made clear that I want to leave Ablepoint because I believe I am being abused, neglected, surveilled and tortured, while being left in poverty and without the support necessary to live with dignity. My continued entrapment raises fundamental questions about safeguarding, provider accountability, conflicts of interest, the proper use of NDIS funding and the obligations of government agencies to respond when a participant explicitly reports that they are unsafe.

I am also concerned about the possibility of undisclosed public expenditure or a coordinated use of government resources to protect individuals from accountability. I have described this as a massive black budget because I want the question of funding, expenditure and institutional involvement investigated. I cannot establish the existence or amount of such a budget merely from the circumstances I have experienced, but I demand that any relevant financial records, authorisations, contracts and expenditure be independently examined.

From my perspective, this represents the possibility of an extraordinary misuse of taxpayer money in connection with what I regard as Shorten’s personal vendetta against someone whom he should have been concerned to protect in his capacity as a public official. Instead of receiving protection, I believe I was subjected to circumstances that threatened my physical safety, financial independence, reputation and ability to live an ordinary life.

I survived. I healed without the assistance of a lawyer, a police officer, a politician or anyone else whom I had expected to help me. I survived circumstances in which I believed that every avenue of assistance had been closed and every person I trusted had betrayed me. I rebuilt my life through my own determination, education, faith and commitment to documenting the truth.

My continued existence, my recovery and the evidence I have published are the reasons I refuse to disappear. I believe the documentary record raises serious questions that deserve answers. I am not asking anyone to accept every allegation without examination. I am asking for an independent investigation, access to the original evidence, transparent answers and accountability wherever wrongdoing is established.

I believe that the truth has a power of its own. If the evidence supports my allegations, those responsible must answer for their conduct. If particular claims cannot be substantiated, they must be identified honestly as such. What I will not accept is the continued denial of my humanity, the suppression of my testimony or the assumption that someone with political power is beyond scrutiny.

I am still here. I survived what I believed I was not supposed to survive, and I have placed my documentary record in public view. I believe that my mere existence, my recovery and my refusal to remain silent challenge the conduct I have described and illuminate questions of institutional power, accountability and corruption that must no longer be ignored.
