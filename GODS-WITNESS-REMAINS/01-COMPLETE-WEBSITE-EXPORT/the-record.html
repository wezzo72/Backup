The declaration

I refused to die in the ashes of human betrayal.

But I survived.

The darkness and the rebuilding

I looked directly into my own darkness. And I am not merely the person who survived. I am the person who continued to dream, who found the strength to heal, who rebuilt without the rescue that never came, and who still believes that a better life is possible.

I have known the darkness of betrayal, but I will not make betrayal my identity. I have experienced the consequences of abandonment, but I will not abandon myself. What is important

1. The archive is real, and large. The public repository and the Official Government Documents Register exist. The register describes 395 entries and later passes over thousands of files. The Commonwealth Ombudsman (16 September 2022), the Federal Court Registry (27 March 2023), the Department of Social Services (18 July 2023) and the Department of Health and Aged Care (19 October 2023) each recorded a decision not to allocate a disclosure, on the terms the register describes. Part VIII — The human consequences, kept separate

He has published an account of having no reliable access to money, food, medicine, transport, a telephone and care, including while a plan with a large total existed on paper. Part IX — Legal questions left as questions

Whether each disclosure was assessed by the right officer, under the Act as it stood, with adequate reasons, is a question for the full letters. Non-allocation is not exoneration and not proof of a breach.

Whether the October 2024 revision lawfully changed the 2022 refusal depends on the request, the searches, the five documents and the deletions. The existence of a revision does not decide that.

Whether drop-in support in June 2023, or the 2025 plan, met a duty depends on the rules then in force, the assessment, and what was actually provided.

Nothing on this page declares conduct criminal, negligent, or a breach of duty. Those words need the obligation, the act, the evidence, and the answer. Unfair is not the same as unlawful. Unlawful is not the same as a conspiracy.

Employment. Comcare and the Tribunal decided a compensation definition of employee, adversely, as the register describes. Freedom of information. “No documents” in 2022, then five documents in 2024, is a change that has to be reconciled. It is not, by itself, proof of concealment.

Plan and need. A large approval and a report of unmet need can both be true. Matching each funded line to a roster is the test. Neither side of that pair proves a breach on its own.
