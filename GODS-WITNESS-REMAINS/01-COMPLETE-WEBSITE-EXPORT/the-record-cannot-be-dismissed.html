The record can no longer be dismissed without being examined

The inevitability of the record

The Witness Remains — The Great Reckoning.

A foundational public declaration and a fifty-exhibit documentary directory: the testimony preserved, the questions exposed, and a permanent opportunity for independent examination.

Author: Dr Richard William McLean, publicly known as Barran Dodger and Barran Resonance Dodger.

Why this record matters

This brings together a published testimony, a public documentary archive, an organised register of official records, and fifty principal exhibits. The account is no longer only the author’s recollection. A reader can open the letter.

This is not a demand that every allegation be accepted. It is a demand that the evidence be examined before a conclusion is reached, and that the human being behind the paper is not overlooked. Safety, accommodation, financial independence, disability support, and Crystal, sit at the centre of that purpose.

Primary publication: gods-witness-remains.grok.me. Repository: wezzo72/Barrandodger. Published archive: wezzo72.github.io/Barrandodger. Rebuilt archive: barrandodger-rebuild.

I. The testimony exists outside one office

An institution may dispute a complaint. A department may close a file. None of those acts removes a document that has already been published. The examination can begin with the documents, not only with memory.

II. The chronology has to be read together

This site identifies 1,333 official government documents and related correspondence: the official records. One letter can look small on its own. A refusal can look final until the request, the evidence and the later letters are placed beside it. The other indexes are the evidence cross-links and the inventory. An index is not a proof.

III. Evidence is not the same thing as an allegation

The distinctions used here are these.

  • Documented fact: what an authenticated document actually establishes.
  • Official record: what an identified institution formally recorded.
  • Author’s testimony: what I report experiencing or witnessing.
  • Author’s allegation: what I say happened and identify for investigation.
  • Inference: what I believe follows when several records are read together.
  • Unverified matter: what still needs authentication or corroboration.

Allegations concerning Bill Shorten, Houd Meraby, Ben and other named people are not presented here as criminal findings. A document does not prove every interpretation placed on it. The absence of an investigation does not prove that a reported event never happened. The response is examination. See also the formal police report of 18 November 2026 and LECC CASE202512395.

IV. The archive is a public historical reference

The repository holds original files, indexes, hashes and timestamp records. A hash can show whether a file has changed. A verified timestamp can show that particular bytes existed. Neither proves that the words inside are true. The archive is not a court. The preservation page on this site is the permanent testimony.

V. The humanitarian dimension cannot be ignored

Behind the chronology is a life. I have asked for accommodation, food, financial independence, medication, a telephone, transport, disability support, legal assistance and safety. Crystal’s food, veterinary care and a safe place are part of the same appeal. An archive cannot feed a person. The appeal is the humanitarian SOS — Crystal and me.

VI. Accountability is a matter of the record and of responsibility

The matters on the table include complaint handling, disability-support administration, financial management, accommodation, correspondence, public-interest disclosures and the treatment of a person who reported serious concerns. Where a failure is established, accountability should follow. Where the evidence does not support an allegation, that must be said accurately.

VII. The record can outlast the people

Ministers change. Files are closed. A preserved record can still be read. Disagreement should happen against the documents, not only against competing memories. Errors, when found, are to be corrected in the open.

VIII. The public-interest question

The wider questions are whether process can replace investigation, whether complaints can be redirected without the substance being addressed, whether financial management can remove practical independence, whether funded support can exist without the assistance being delivered, and whether a psychiatric history can be used to avoid the evidence. Those questions stand even if not every allegation is later substantiated.

IX. Why the witness remains

My faith is part of the testimony. The passages I place here are Isaiah 54:17, Romans 8:31, Psalm 118:17 and Revelation 12:11. They are convictions. They are not a substitute for the documents. The biblical examination of conscience and the forgotten record is The Mordecai Declaration.

X. What is inevitable

I do not claim a particular legal outcome. I do not claim that every allegation is proved. What has already happened is this: the testimony is published, the archive exists, the registers exist, the chronology can be examined, and the humanitarian appeal can be read. The inevitable consequence of preserving a record is that the opportunity to examine it continues. Silence, ridicule or assumption is not an examination.

I ask for truth, fairness, safety, dignity and independent examination. I ask that Crystal and I be permitted to live with the security every person is owed.

XI. To the reader

Investigators, journalists, lawyers, human-rights organisations, government offices, researchers and the public are invited to open the source. The invitation is not to accept a predetermined narrative.

How to verify a file

  1. Open the file. Do not stop at the filename.
  2. Name who issued it, the date, the recipient, and whether it is an original, a copy, a transcript or a later compilation.
  3. Read the operative words, the reasons, the reference numbers and the limits.
  4. Compare it with the related exhibit: the PID assessment with the non-allocation notices, the plan with the SIL refusal, the section 122 record with the vehicle decision, IR8415987 with the hospital and complaint papers.
  5. Classify it: official record, author submission, private communication, testimony, analytical report, or an independent finding. Do not mix those classes.
