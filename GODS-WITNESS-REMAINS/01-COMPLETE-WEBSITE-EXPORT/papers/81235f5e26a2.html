The declaration

I refused to die in the ashes of human betrayal.

But I survived.

The darkness and the rebuilding

I looked directly into my own darkness. And I am not merely the person who survived. I am the person who continued to dream, who found the strength to heal, who rebuilt without the rescue that never came, and who still believes that a better life is possible.

I have known the darkness of betrayal, but I will not make betrayal my identity. I have experienced the consequences of abandonment, but I will not abandon myself.
